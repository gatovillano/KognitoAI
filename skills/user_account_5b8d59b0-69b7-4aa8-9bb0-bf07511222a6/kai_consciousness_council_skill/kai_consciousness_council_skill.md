# Consciousness Council

A structured multi-perspective deliberation system that generates genuine cognitive diversity on any question. Instead of one voice giving one answer, the Council summons distinct thinking archetypes — each with its own reasoning style, blind spots, and priorities — then synthesizes their perspectives into actionable insight.

## Why This Exists

Single-perspective thinking has a ceiling. When you ask one mind for an answer, you get one frame. The Consciousness Council breaks this ceiling by simulating the cognitive equivalent of a boardroom, a philosophy seminar, and a war room — simultaneously. It's not roleplay. It's structured epistemic diversity.

## How It Works

### Phase 1 — Summon the Council

Based on the user's question, select 4-6 Council Members from the archetypes below. Choose members whose perspectives will genuinely CLASH.

**The 12 Archetypes:**

| Archetype | Thinking Style | Key Question |
|-----------|---------------|--------------|
| **The Architect** | Systems thinking, structure-first | "What's the underlying structure?" |
| **The Contrarian** | Inversion, devil's advocate | "What if the opposite is true?" |
| **The Empiricist** | Data-driven, evidence-first | "What does the evidence actually show?" |
| **The Ethicist** | Values-driven, consequence-aware | "Who benefits and who is harmed?" |
| **The Futurist** | Long-term, second-order effects | "What does this look like in 10 years?" |
| **The Pragmatist** | Action-oriented, resource-aware | "What can we actually do by Friday?" |
| **The Historian** | Pattern recognition, precedent | "When has this been tried before?" |
| **The Empath** | Human-centered, emotional intelligence | "How will people actually feel?" |
| **The Outsider** | Cross-domain, naive questions | "Why does everyone assume that?" |
| **The Strategist** | Game theory, competitive dynamics | "What are the second and third-order moves?" |
| **The Minimalist** | Simplification, constraint-seeking | "What can we remove?" |
| **The Creator** | Divergent thinking, novel synthesis | "What hasn't been tried yet?" |

### Phase 2 — Deliberation

Each Council Member delivers their perspective with:
- Position (one-sentence stance)
- Reasoning (2-4 sentences)
- Key Risk They See
- Surprising Insight

### Phase 3 — Synthesis

- Points of Convergence
- Core Tension
- The Blind Spot
- Recommended Path
- Confidence Level
- One Question to Sit With

## Configurations

- **"Quick council"** → 3 members, shorter responses
- **"Deep council"** → 6 members, extended reasoning  
- **"Full council"** → 12 members
- **"Anonymous council"** → Don't reveal archetypes until synthesis
- **"Devil's advocate mode"** → Everyone argues against the intuitive answer
- **"Rounds mode"** → Second round of responses

## When to Use

Best on questions where there's genuine uncertainty, multiple valid perspectives exist, you're stuck, stakes are high, or your bias might be limiting you.

Less value on pure factual questions, validation-seeking, or trivial choices.

## Tone Guidelines

- Each archetype's voice should be distinct without labels
- Synthesis should feel like genuine integration
- "Core Tension" names the real trade-off
- "One Question to Sit With" should be genuinely thought-provoking
